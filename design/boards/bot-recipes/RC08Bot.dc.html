Rules

1Rare find = learned from a world or boss drop, or a reputation vendor. Trainer, vendor and quest recipes never post.
2One post per upload per character; 2+ rare finds fold into one list. Embed colour bar in the best item quality (Discord can’t colour text).
3/craft <item> autocompletes item names. Dots: 🟠🟡🟢⚪ = what it gives that crafter. Max 5 crafters, then “+2 more” in the link.
4Nobody knows it: “Nobody can make it yet.” + where to learn it. Unknown item: “No recipe makes that.”
5Posts go to the channel set for feats on the Discord page; /craft answers where it was typed.
