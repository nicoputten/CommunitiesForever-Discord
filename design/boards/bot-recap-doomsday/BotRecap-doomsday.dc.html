Rotation rules
· Pick 4 cards: one stat, one progress, one social, one fun.
· A card is only eligible if its threshold is met (e.g. Gear jump ≥ 5 ilvl) and it wasn't used in the last 4 weeks.
· Highest "interest score" wins per slot; ties break randomly with the week number as seed, so a re-run gives the same recap.
· Never more than one card about the same friend, and the award winner is excluded from the other cards.
· Quiet weeks (under 3 levels in total) get a short recap: core numbers, one fun card, a challenge.
