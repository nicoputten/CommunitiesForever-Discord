Doombringers bot · what it posts · runs

Planned runs and reminders go to #doombringers-runs. Times are Discord timestamps, so everyone sees their own time zone. Posts use display names and full character names; never BattleTags or Discord IDs. Milestones and the weekly recap are unchanged.

Why “via /reload”: the game can't reach the internet. After ticking “Also post on Discord”, the player reloads; the uploader sees the saved file, waits 5 s and uploads; the site posts this in about 10–15 s. Discord answers reach the asker after their next sync and show as answered on Discord.
